# Easily Uncomplicated

Simple on purpose.

We make easy-to-use apps that do one job well. Your data and your privacy come first.

## Privacy

- Everything you enter is saved on your own device, in your browser. Nothing you type leaves it.
- There are no accounts, no logins and no ads.

## Syncing

Because your data stays on your device, the apps can't connect with each other or share data across devices and platforms yet. Secure, optional accounts may be added later to make that possible.
